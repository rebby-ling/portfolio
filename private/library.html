The challenge

The NLB Mobile App offers thousands of books, articles, videos, and reading trails. However, content discovery relied heavily on manual search and traditional categorisation, resulting in missed opportunities for user engagement.

NLB wanted to explore new ways to surface relevant content—especially for casual, inspiration-driven exploration—while preserving existing information architecture patterns.

My role & responsibilities

As the lead interaction designer, I drove the design process and helped align the team around clear, testable concepts that informed the final recommendations.

  • Designed and prototyped multiple content exploration concepts
  • Improved clarity of “For You”, genre-based browsing, and smart lists
  • Developed UX flows for simple and advanced discovery behaviours
  • Collaborated closely with NLB product owners to refine scope
  • Produced mobile-ready UI components to support future testing
