The challenge

ePlanner houses diverse modules—including Site Simulation, Wind Analysis, and Urban Synth. Each module was built at different times, by different engineers, and with different UX approaches. This led to inconsistent components, disjointed navigation patterns, and confusing workflows across the platform.

As planning decisions involve policy makers, analysts, and technical specialists, clarity and consistency were essential to reduce cognitive load and improve workflow efficiency.

My role & responsibilities

My work focused on strengthening ePlanner’s design foundation through system-level improvements, user research, and collaborative design delivery. These efforts led to a more consistent interface, clearer workflows, and a roadmap informed by real user insights.

  • Created unified UX flows across technical and planning modules
  • Improved form-heavy workflows and information hierarchy
  • Defined UX patterns for navigation, table interactions, and side panels
  • Worked closely with URA’s engineers during grooming & QA cycles
  • Introduced structured design governance across multidisciplinary teams

User research & testing

While there was some user testing done by the internal team previously, there was no formalised process and quantitative way to properly capture the feedback they received. I thus helped to formalise the User Testing process within URA through the establishment of UX research frameworks that better quantified and captured key user feedback. The frameworks could be customised to fit the team's need.
