The challenge

BR2S consolidates multiple ACRA platforms into one unified experience. Each user group— business owners, corporate service providers, and government officers—has different needs, permissions, and mental models.

The challenge was to redesign workflows that were long, highly regulated, and filled with branching logic, while still ensuring clarity, transparency, and efficiency across all roles.

My role & responsibilities

  • Mapped and redesigned key business registration workflows
  • Created interaction patterns for dashboards, submissions, and task queues
  • Simplified long, multi-step forms using clearer hierarchy & progressive disclosure
  • Worked closely with BAs and policy teams to translate regulations into user flows
  • Contributed UI components and guidelines to the Design System

Interaction model

BR2S needed an interaction model that could support the diverse mental models of small business owners, corporate service providers (CSPs), and government officers. While each role performed different tasks, their journeys shared common patterns around task management, multi-step submissions, entity switching, and validation. We found that Bizfile users are task-oriented and work reactive users who are extrinsically motivated only to get work done. Most of the time tasks can be timebound and critical.

Guiding principles

Contextual & progressive disclosure

Show relevant and necessary information up front without overwhelming users with too much at one go

Purposeful actions

Provide a clear explanation of the purpose and implications of each action the user can take

Helping hand

Provide assistance to the users, so that it limits human errors and users are able to recover from the errors

Next best course of action

Promptly communicate the status and outcome of user's actions, recommending next best actions where possible
