Zoff Consumer App

Zoff is a popular Japanese eyewear brand expanding into Southeast Asia. I designed new app experiences to improve browsing, eyewear try-ons, and store-to-online conversion. The goal: make eyewear exploration simple, mobile-first, and delightful for everyday shoppers.

The challenge

Eyewear shopping is deeply visual. Users need clarity around frame shape, sizing, lens options, and how each pair suits their face. Zoff wanted an app experience that supports fast browsing, confident selection, and easy comparison—without overwhelming the user.

The challenge: deliver a polished, intuitive mobile-first flow that supports both casual browsing and intent-driven buyers.

My role & responsibilities

For this project, I was a key designer that helped to spearhead the design process for the other designers. Some of my main responsibilities include:

  • Designed product discovery flows optimized for mobile shopping
  • Improved filtering, categorisation, and visual browsing patterns
  • Refined AR try-on integration and lens selection steps
  • Created interactive prototypes for usability reviews
  • Built UI components for consistency across screens

Improving eyewear discovery

As this was Zoff's new foray into the South-east Asian market, we conducted market research across Malaysia, Singapore and Hong Kong to find out what strategy would be ideal to tackle these markets. We conducted competitor analysis, user interviews and identified existing mindsets and mental models of eyewear shoppers. The findings and insights we got helped to inform the client on the best ways to enter these markets in a way that could maximise their sales.

The design system

I took ownership of the design system for the Zoff mobile app, creating a unified component library that streamlined design and development. From product cards and filters to page-level templates and visual rules, I defined the patterns that shaped the core user experience. This improved design velocity, ensured UI consistency across modules, and made it easier for the team to test and launch new features quickly.
